## The design in one paragraph

Both tops stand 3 cm off their walls (was 5; the VIVO clamp back needs about 1.5 cm). The tops sit on two ALEX units (centred under the far ends, 3.5 cm of top showing all round) via four round 4 in adjustable feet per unit (plates screwed to the top, 5 cm pads sitting in printed locating rings screwed to the ALEX tops, 5 cm in from each ALEX corner), and on six IKEA OLOV legs set to 80.2 cm, so the surface is 83.0 cm. Two 2 × 2 × 3/16 in hot-rolled steel angle rails (cut to 213 cm by the supplier) run under the front edges 12 cm back and are joined at the inside corner by two 5 mm gusseted 90° strut fittings (1-5/8 in channel size, two holes per leg), one each side of rail B's end, bolted to the vertical legs with 3/8-16 bolts. Scott sits at the inside corner facing the diagonal; a VIVO STAND-V300CU clamps to top A's two wall edges at 40 cm from the corner with the crossbar on the diagonal, carrying a Samsung Odyssey G5 32 in (centre, 1000R curved, 71 × 44 cm, VESA 75, about 5 kg; it replaced the Smart M7 on 21 Oct 2026) and two Acer XZ320QU 31.5 in 1500R curved monitors along the walls, all three in landscape, an Edifier R1280DBs under each Acer, an IKEA ELLOVEN drawer under the M7, an 80 × 30 cm deskmat with a NuPhy Air75 V2 centred on the diagonal, a HYTE Y60 standing on its I/O end on a printed pedestal over ALEX 1, an ELYONA swing-arm lamp beside the Y60 at (183, 20), a wooden headset stand at (28, 140) and a plant at (32, 298) on top B.

## How `index.html` is built

- `box(x0,x1,y0,y1,z0,z1,mat)` and `cyl(r0,r1,h,mat,x,y,z)` are the two helpers; `box()` positions the mesh at the centre of the given extents. When you rotate a part, build it inside a `THREE.Group`, position the group, rotate the group.
- `setup` group holds everything that is not structure (chair, monitors, PC, speakers, lamp, mat); the "Chair & setup" toggle hides it.
- Labels are canvas sprites made with `label(text, x, y, z, scale)` and live in `labels`.
- Camera presets are in `views`; the orbit is hand-rolled (drag rotate, wheel zoom, right-drag pan).
- The Acers are slices of a 150 cm radius cylinder (1500R). Their side arms run from the pole along the wall to the panel centre's x (or z), then turn forward into the VESA plate; keep them behind the panels.
- Theme: CSS tokens on `:root`, dark by default, light under `prefers-color-scheme: light`.
