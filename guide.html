0 · How to read the numbers

Every position is a pair (x, y) in centimetres measured from the back-left corner of top A: x runs along the back wall, y runs along the side wall. Top A covers x 0–249, y 0–65. Top B covers x 0–65, y 65–314. The two tops meet on the line y = 65. Both tops stand 3 cm off their walls.

Your seat is at the inside corner of the L, facing the corner diagonally. The three monitors wrap around that corner; your knees swing through the open space under the corner square and the first 55 cm of each wing. Nothing is allowed to stand in that sweep, which is why all six legs are on the wall edges and the front edges are carried by steel rails instead.

Plan view from above. Gold circles are legs, with their 11 cm plates drawn to size. Small circles are the ALEX feet. Grey strips are the rails, 12 cm back from the front edges. The dashed area is the knee sweep.

When a top is face down on the blanket you are looking at its underside, so the layout appears mirrored. Sections 3 and 4 draw each top exactly as you will see it face down, with the measuring edges marked. Measure from those two edges and the drawing matches.

1 · Parts and tools

Fasteners (every screw, with how deep it goes)

The tops are 28 mm particleboard with a laminate face. No fastener goes deeper than 21 mm into a top. Every depth below respects that; if your tops measure 27 mm, shorten each screw into the top by 1 mm.

2 · Steel: drill the rails on the bench

Two pieces of 2 × 2 × 3/16 in hot-rolled angle, each 213 cm (84 in), arrive cut. One leg of the angle goes flat against the underside of the top (the flat leg); the other hangs down toward the floor (the vertical leg). All screw holes go in the flat leg, 19 mm from its free edge. The bolt holes for the corner fittings are not drilled on the bench; they are marked through the fittings in place (section 6).

Rail A. Stations are measured from the wall end. The flat leg is shown; holes on the line 19 mm from the free edge.

Rail B. Stations are measured from the corner end. Holes 1 and 2 land in top A; holes 3 to 10 in top B.

  1. Label the ends with tape: rail A wall end, rail B corner end. File both ends of both rails.
  2. With a combination square, scribe the hole line 19 mm from the free edge of the flat leg along the full length of each rail.
  3. Mark the stations from the labelled end with a tape measure, centre-punch each mark.
  4. Drill 7/32 in at every mark, low speed, a drop of oil on each. Deburr both sides with the file or a larger bit turned by hand.
  5. Degrease with acetone, scuff the mill scale with 120 grit, two light coats of flat black enamel, 24 h to cure. Holes clear themselves; mask nothing.

3 · Top A, face down

Lay top A face down on a blanket. Find the end that will go against the side wall (that is x = 0) and the long edge that will go against the back wall (y = 0). Put a strip of tape on each and label them. Every mark below is measured from those two edges. The drawing is what you see from above the face-down top: x grows to the left.

Top A underside as seen face down. Solid gold circles: OLOV plates (11 cm). Grey squares: feet plates (6 cm). Grey strip: rail A. Hatched: VIVO clamp zones (keep clear). Pale bars: the two loose mending plates that pad the clamp jaws. Blue bars: the two joining plates, half on this top.

  1. Legs L1, L2, L3. Mark each centre. Set the OLOV plate on the mark, scribe through its four holes, pilot 1/8 in, 15 mm deep (tape flag on the bit at 15 mm). Drive the IKEA M4 × 16 mm screws. Pre-set each leg to 80.2 cm from plate face to foot.
  2. Feet F1–F4. Mark each centre; the 6 cm plate sits centred on it, its edges parallel to the top's edges. Scribe the four plate holes, pilot 1/8 in, 15 mm deep, drive #8 × 5/8 in screws. Pad pointing away from the top. Wind each pad out until the foot measures 10.2 cm overall.
  3. Clamp zones. Nothing goes in x 31–39 along the back edge (9 cm in) or y 31–39 along the left end (9 cm in). Lay the two spare mending plates loose where drawn; they are placed between the clamp jaw and the top in section 7, no screws.
  4. Rail A. Scribe the rail line at y = 53 from x = 1 to 214. Clamp the rail with its vertical leg's outer face on that line, flat leg toward the back edge (covering y 48–53), wall end flush with x = 0 plus 1 cm (the rail starts at x = 1). Through each of the nine holes drill a 1/8 in pilot 20 mm into the top: tape flag 25 mm from the bit tip (4.8 mm steel + 20 mm). Drive #10 × 1 in screws with the Robertson bit on the 3 in extension so the chuck clears the vertical leg; stop when the head seats.
  5. Joining plates wait for section 6; mark their outlines now so nothing else lands there.

4 · Top B, face down

Lay top B face down. The end that will butt against top A is y = 65; the long edge that goes against the side wall is x = 0. Label both. The drawing is again as seen from above the face-down top, x growing to the left.

Top B underside as seen face down. The rail's corner end projects 12 cm past the joint end; that 12 cm will lie under top A.

  1. Legs L4, L5, L6 and feet F5–F8 exactly as on top A.
  2. Rail B. Scribe the rail line at x = 53 from the joint end (y = 65) to y = 266. Clamp the rail with its vertical leg's outer face on that line, flat leg toward the wall edge (x 48–53), and its corner end projecting 12 cm past the joint end (it will sit against rail A under top A). Pilot and screw holes 3 to 10 only, same 25 mm flag, eight screws. Holes 1 and 2 are driven into top A in section 6.

5 · ALEX units and locating rings

The feet pads do not touch bare laminate. Each pad drops into a printed ring screwed to the ALEX top, so the top cannot slide on the units and the units brace the whole desk against racking.

ALEX 1 top (36 × 58), drawers toward you. Ring centres 5 cm in from each corner.

ALEX 2 top (58 × 36), door toward you. Same rule.

  1. Print eight rings: 51 mm bore, 3 mm wall, 10 mm tall, no floor, three countersunk holes on the wall for #6 × 5/8 in screws. PETG or ABS. Measure your pad first; the bore is pad + 1 mm.
  2. Mark the four ring centres on each ALEX top, 5 cm in from each corner. Set each ring on its mark, scribe the three holes, pilot 5/64 in 12 mm deep, drive the screws.
  3. Place the units: ALEX 1 at x 209.5–245.5, y 3.5–61.5 with the drawers facing you; ALEX 2 at x 3.5–61.5, y 274.5–310.5 with the door facing you. Both 3 cm off their walls. Painter's tape on the floor at x = 209.5 and y = 274.5 saves guessing.

6 · Standing it up (two people)

  1. Top A. Lift it onto L1–L3 with the four pads dropping into the rings on ALEX 1. Lift and set; the rings do not allow a push. Final position: 3 cm off both walls. Level along x and along y. Adjust the OLOV feet and the round feet until it reads flat and all four pads bear with no rock. Target 83.0 cm at every corner.
  2. Top B. Lift onto L4–L6 with its pads in the rings on ALEX 2, its end against A's front edge, 3 cm off the side wall. Adjust until the two surfaces are flush at the joint: run a fingertip across; shim the legs, never the plates.
  3. Joining plates. From below, screw P1 and P2 across the joint: each plate centred on the joint line, long axis across it, two #10 × 5/8 in screws with washers in each top, pilots 1/8 in, 12 mm deep. Use the plate's holes nearest 4 cm and 9 cm from the joint on each side.
  4. Rail B meets rail A. Push rail B's corner end against rail A's vertical leg. Drill holes 1 and 2 into top A (25 mm flag) and screw.
  5. Corner fittings. Hold one 5 mm gusseted strut fitting in the outside corner where the two vertical legs meet: one leg flat on rail A's vertical leg toward the far end, the other on rail B's outer face, gusset facing the open quadrant. Hold the second in the inside corner: one leg on rail A's vertical leg toward the wall, the other on the inner face of rail B's vertical leg, gusset under B's flat leg. Both centred 29 mm below the underside of the top; the inside one must clear B's flat leg above and the heads of screws 1 and 2. Centre-punch through all eight holes, remove both fittings, drill the marks 7/16 in (four in rail A, two in rail B where the marks coincide). Refit. Rail A: four 3/8-16 × 1 in bolts, washer under head, washer and nylon-insert nut inside the leg. Rail B: two 3/8-16 × 1½ in bolts through fitting, leg and fitting. Snug, not cranked.

The inside corner in plan view (x right, y down, as in every other drawing). Rail B's end is sandwiched between the two fittings; bolt centres are 29 mm below the top's underside, along the rail wherever the fitting's holes fall.

7 · Monitor mount and what goes where

The two VIVO clamp positions depend on the crossbar's real pole spacing (section 9). The numbers below assume 42 cm; if the crossbar measures 54–58 cm collar to collar, both clamps move out to about 43–46 cm from the corner and nothing else changes.
  1. Lay a spare mending plate flat on the underside at each clamp zone. Fit the VIVO lower clamps using the manual's Option 1 holes (2 and 4), since the 28 mm top plus plate is under 60 mm.
  2. Clamp 1 centred at x = 35 on A's back edge; clamp 2 centred at y = 35 on A's left end edge. The clamp backs sit in the 3 cm wall gap.
  3. Slide the crossbar onto the poles as the spacing guide (VIVO step 3), tighten the clamps, then refit the crossbar at monitor height (VIVO step 6).
  4. Hang the screens and set the heights in the table below. Toe the Acers in about 15° so their near ends sit 2 cm from the M7's ends.

8 · Final checks

  • Top surface 83.0 cm at all six outer corners, ±3 mm.
  • A level across the joint reads flat both ways; a fingertip finds no step.
  • Push the inside corner down hard: it gives 2–3 mm and springs back, no creak.
  • Push the desk sideways along each wall: no sway at the top beyond a few millimetres, no movement of a top on its ALEX.
  • No screw tip visible or felt as a bump on either laminate face.
  • Knees clear: sit at the corner, slide in, nothing within reach of a knee or thigh. The rails' lower edge is 75.1 cm from the floor, 12 cm back from the edge.
  • Both ALEX fronts open fully without touching a foot or a leg.
  • Retighten every screw after two weeks.

9 · Still to measure

  • VIVO crossbar, collar centre to collar centre; pole centre to desk edge; how far the clamp projects behind the edge (the 3 cm gap assumes under 2.5 cm).
  • Round feet load rating (design wants 50 kg or more each); pad diameter with calipers before printing the rings.
  • ALEX top-panel thickness where the ring screws go (expect 16 mm or more).
  • Each top's thickness with calipers (expect 28 mm).
