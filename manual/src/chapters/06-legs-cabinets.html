Measure your ALEX units first, with and without their stock feet. The drawings take an ALEX as 70 cm tall, which is what makes the feet 102 mm between the ALEX top and the underside at 80.2 cm. If a unit is not 70.0 cm tall, nothing changes here; levelling in chapter G takes it up.
2

Hold each foot loose in your hand, plate and all, before anything is screwed. Wind its pad out until the printed foot height gauge (102.0 mm inside) just slides over it from the side with no rock. Fig. F-10 (a) Rail A in section, the top face down, so the vertical leg points up (mm, except the 12 cm). The 25 RAIL puck stands on the flat leg: the 1/8 in pilot goes 20 mm into the wood. (b) Rail B on top B, face down: stand at B's far end, joint end away from you, x grows to the left. Its corner end projects 12 cm past the joint end; holes 1 and 2 (open) lie in the projection.
4

Rail A. Clamp it on its line, flat leg against the top, wall end at x = 1 cm. Hook a rail setback gauge over the front edge near each end and push the rail against both (12 cm from the front edge to the vertical leg's outer face); check the middle with one. Through each rail hole drill a 1/8 in pilot with the 25 RAIL puck standing on the flat leg (20 mm into the wood). Drive the two end screws first, re-check the 12 cm, then the rest: H03 #10 × 1 in Robertson on the #2 bit in the 3 in extension (the chuck would hit the vertical leg otherwise), until each head seats. Nine screws.

5

Rail B. Clamp it on its line with the corner end projecting 12 cm past top B's joint end. Pilot and screw holes 3 to 10 only, the same way: eight screws. Holes 1 and 2 go into top A in chapter H.

The printed rail end caps go on later, once the desk stands; they are handed: cap A fits rail A's far end, cap B fits rail A's wall end and rail B's far end. Rail B's corner end is bolted to rail A and takes no cap.

While it stands 5 cm out, top B's far end reaches 319 cm from the back wall: the side wall must run at least 322 cm. If it does not, stop and ask before chapter G.

No rings yet: they are fitted in chapter G by tracing the pads (a 50 mm pad in a 52 mm ring has only about 1 mm each side).

Optional ALEX feet (decided at the start of this chapter): fit them now, before the cabinets stand. Unscrew the stock feet, lay the unit on its back, hook the corner template over each bottom corner and use its inner three holes (r 14.5 mm). Pilot 5/64 in, 12 mm deep with the 12 ALEX puck; three #6 × 5/8 flat heads per foot down the wells, four feet per unit, all the same height.
!
MEASURE ON HARDWARE MEASURE ON HARDWARE
Before fitting ALEX feet, measure the ALEX bottom panel at an edge. 14 mm or more is fine: the 12 ALEX pilot is 12 mm deep and the #6 × 5/8 screw reaches only 9.9 mm into the panel through the foot's 6 mm roof, so 4 mm remains at 14 mm. Under 14 mm: do not fit them. The top panel measured 15.9 mm, so the bottom is likely similar and likely fine.
