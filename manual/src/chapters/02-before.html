B · Before you begin

Read this chapter once before you pick up a drill. Budget a weekend: the steel on day one, the assembly on day two.

Safety

Fig. B-1 · The five rules that protect the tops and you.
!
NEVER A FASTENER DEEPER THAN 21 MM INTO A TOP
The EKBACKEN tops are 28 mm particleboard with a thin laminate skin. A screw or pilot that reaches the skin lifts it into a bump you will see and feel for ever. Every screw in this manual stops at least 7 mm short of the top face (28 − 21). If your tops measure 27 mm instead of 28, shorten every screw into the top by 1 mm. If you ever substitute a screw, keep this rule. Chapter D shows every depth.
  • Laminate protection. Spread a blanket on the floor first. The tops lie face down on it, laminate on the blanket, for every marking and drilling step. Never slide or drag steel (rails, fittings, the clamp plates) across the laminate: lift it on and off.
  • Eye protection for every hole drilled in steel; drill at low speed with cutting oil. Gloves while you deburr and file the steel: drilled edges are sharp.
  • Two people to turn a top over and stand it on its legs (2490 long). A helper is also needed to slide the finished desk off and back to the walls.

Work area

Fig. B-2 · Floor needed: 2520 × 3170, including the 30 mm gaps to both walls.

The finished desk needs 2520 × 3170 mm of clear floor in the corner: each top is 2490 long and stands 30 mm off its wall. MEASURE ON HARDWARE Measure both walls (wall to wall) and the baseboard thickness before you start.

You also need floor space for one top at a time lying face down on the blanket (2490 × 650 plus room to kneel all round), and a bench with two clamps for drilling the two 2130 mm steel rails. All steel is drilled on the bench, never under the desk.

Clear the corner before chapter G: the ALEX units are placed by the walls before the tops go on.

Protective equipment

  • Safety glasses or goggles: every hole in steel, and opening the 7/16 in holes.
  • Work gloves: deburring, filing and handling the cut rail ends.
  • A blanket large enough for a whole top (2490 × 650).

Printed tools (P18): print these before you mark anything

All of them are in parts/tools/; parts/README.md has print settings. Print as saved, no supports. Never scale a printed tool in the slicer: its distances are the tool. The lipped and fenced tools print lip-up and are turned over to use.

How sure is each number?

Every dimension in this manual carries one of three confidence labels, or a fourth label that is an instruction to you:

VERIFIEDMeasured on the purchased part, or taken from the part's own geometry file (the printed parts).
SOURCE-DEFINEDDefined by the project documents (the build manual): a design position, not a measurement. It is right by definition; you put the part there.
ASSUMPTION / FIELD CHECKAn engineering estimate or a check done on site. Confirm it before you rely on it.
MEASURE ON HARDWARENot yet measured on your parts. Measure it yourself before the step that uses it, and stop if it differs.
