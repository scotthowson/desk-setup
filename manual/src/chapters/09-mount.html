I · Monitor mount and accessories

The VIVO STAND-V300CU (P12) has two independent C-clamps and no base bar. Both clamp onto top A's own wall edges, never on the joint: clamp 1 centred at x = 40.0 cm on the back edge, clamp 2 centred at y = 40.0 cm on the side-wall end. The crossbar runs on the diagonal between the poles. Each lower jaw bears on a spare steel mending plate (P11), because particleboard crushes under a bare steel jaw over months. Datum: top A's back-left corner (0, 0); x along the back wall, y along the side wall.

Hang the mount

1

Lay the pad plates. Lay a spare mending plate P11 flat on the underside, on each pad outline drawn when top A was marked (chapter D), long side along the edge: x 20.0–45.4 cm, y 0.5–8.1 cm at clamp 1; y 20.0–45.4 cm, x 0.5–8.1 cm at clamp 2. Loose, no screws: the jaw holds it. SOURCE-DEFINED

!
MEASURE ON HARDWARE before hanging the clamps
Top arm reach (about 89 mm) and maximum opening (needs 31 mm or more: 28 mm top + 3 mm plate) are not measured. Open the lower clamp fully on the Option 1 holes and measure the gap with calipers; if it is under 31 mm, stop. The clamp zones are drawn 9 cm deep from the edge; if the reach measures more, keep the zone clear to the measured reach.
2

Hang both lower clamps, finger-tight only. Clamp 1 centred at x = 40.0 cm on A's back edge, clamp 2 centred at y = 40.0 cm on A's side-wall end (Fig. I-1). Use the VIVO sheet's Option 1 holes (2 and 4): top and plate together are 31 mm, under 60 mm. The clamp backs sit in the 3 cm wall gap; the lower pad lands on the plate, 13.5–63.5 mm from the edge (Fig. I-2).

3

Fit the poles and the crossbar as the spacing guide (VIVO sheet step 3). The crossbar measured 50.2 cm centre to centre, so it fixes the pole spacing. Only now tighten both clamps, with the crossbar on. VERIFIED

4

Refit the crossbar at monitor height (VIVO sheet step 6): 40–43 cm above the top (123.0–126.0 cm from the floor).

5

Hang the three screens, all landscape, and set the heights in Fig. I-3. The G5 goes on the centre bracket (VESA 75), screen centred on the diagonal at (36.5, 36.5) cm, 20 cm in front of the crossbar. The Acers go on the side arms, toed in 15°; each Acer's near end ends up about 12 cm from the G5's curved end. The side arms run along the wall behind each panel, then forward into the VESA plate.
