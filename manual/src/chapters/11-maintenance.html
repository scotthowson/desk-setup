NEVER DEEPER THAN 21 MM INTO A TOP
The tops are 28 mm particleboard with a thin laminate skin; a screw reaching the skin lifts it. Replace a screw only with the same size. If a top measured 27 mm, shorten every screw into it by 1 mm (build manual step 0). SOURCE-DEFINED

Corner bolts

Boltssix 3/8-16 × 1-1/2 in hex bolts, grade 5 (H08), washer under head and nut (H09), nylon-insert nuts (H10): four through rail A's vertical leg, two through rail B's leg with a fitting on each side Torque25 N·m (18 ft·lb); without a torque wrench, pull as hard as one hand can on a 15 cm wrench, then stop Whya friction joint: each fitting leg has two 21 × 12 mm slots, centred 29.15 mm and 78.45 mm from the heel VERIFIED; they leave each bolt 2.5 mm of play across and 11.5 mm along, and the rails' 7/16 in holes 1.6 mm, so only the clamping force keeps rail B's corner from settling Ordertighten only with the two surfaces flush and rail B's end touching rail A

The mending plates across the joint align the tops; they do not carry the corner. SOURCE-DEFINED

Troubleshooting

SymptomCauseFix
Corner has dropped; step at the joint grewThe six fitting bolts lost clamping force (friction joint); supports settlingBring the surfaces flush with the OLOV feet and the round feet, never by packing anything under the joining plates. Check rail B's end still touches rail A. Retighten all six bolts to 25 N·m. Write the new step down (Fig. J-5).
Top moves on its ALEX, or a pad spins or rocksA pad is out of its ring or not bearingLift that ALEX end a few millimetres and slide the ALEX unit, not the top, until the pads drop into their rings. Each foot set with the 102 mm foot height gauge, no rock. Level along x and y; re-check 83.0 cm at the corners (Figs. J-1, J-4).
Printed ring cracked or its screws looseRing wall overloaded by a sideways pushInspect at two weeks. A crack: reprint the ring (parts/structure/foot-ring.stl), PETG or ABS, flange-down: 52 mm bore, 3 mm wall, 75 × 2.5 mm flange. Refit with #6 × 5/8 in flat-head screws (H06), 5/64 in pilots 12 mm deep (12 ALEX puck). Never run the desk with a ring missing.
Leg moves or wobblesOLOV plate screws looseRetighten the five M4 × 16 mm screws (H01). A screw that spins: repair the hole (below), re-pilot 1/8 in, 15 mm deep (15 LEG puck). Re-set the OLOV to 80.2 cm, or the ALEX-feet table's number.
Monitor clamp creepsClamp loosened over timeRetighten the clamp with the crossbar fitted. Keep the steel plate (P11) flat on its outline under the jaw. Never add a soft pad under the jaw.
