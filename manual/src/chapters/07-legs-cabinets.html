4

Drop the corner template over each ALEX corner, fences against both edges. Start the three ring pilots 3–4 mm through it with the 5/64 in bit, lift it, finish 12 deep with the 12 ALEX puck. Set a ring over the three pilots and drive three H06 #6 × 5/8 flat heads (13 into the ALEX top). Eight rings, 24 screws.

5

Stand ALEX 1 (P03) with its back 65 off the back wall and its near side 2125 from the side wall, drawers toward you. Stand ALEX 2 (P04) with its side 65 off the side wall and its near end 2775 from the back wall, door toward you. Painter's tape on the floor round each one saves guessing.

Once the tops are on, that leaves 35 of top showing all round each cabinet and the tops 30 off the walls. The feet pads drop into the rings when the tops go on; that is what stops the desk sliding on the cabinets, and the cabinets then hold the whole desk against racking.

Optional ALEX feet (decided on page G-1): fit them now, before the cabinets stand. Unscrew the stock feet, lay the unit on its back, hook the corner template over each bottom corner and use its inner three holes (r 14.5). Pilot 5/64 in, 12 deep with the 12 ALEX puck; three #6 × 5/8 flat heads per foot down the wells, four feet per unit, all the same height.
