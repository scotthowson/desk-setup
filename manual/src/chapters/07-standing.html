G · Standing the tops and fitting the rings

Two people. Both tops lie face down with their legs, feet and rails on (chapter F), every OLOV at 80.2 cm and every pad set with the foot height gauge (102 mm). ALEX 1 stands in its place; ALEX 2 stands 5 cm short of its place, its near end 282.5 cm from the back wall. Neither has rings yet: each ring is placed by tracing round its pad.

P01 top A · P02 top B · P03 ALEX 1 · P04 ALEX 2 · P05 OLOV leg · P06 rail A · P07 rail B · P09 foot · P10 ring × 8 H06 #6 × 5/8 in flat head × 24 (3 per ring) Sharp pencil, wall gauge, ALEX corner template, drill guide (SET tower, 12 ALEX puck), 5/64 in bit, level, tape measure, blanket
1
Stand top A on the bare ALEX 1. With a helper, turn top A over and stand it on its legs with its four pads on the bare ALEX top: lower the ALEX end first, then the leg end. Push the ALEX unit, never the top, until 3.5 cm of top shows all round the cabinet and the wall gauge's ALEX 65 leg (6.5 cm) just fits between the back wall and the ALEX.
Fig. G-1 · Top A seen from the room, looking at the back wall; x grows to the right (1:26). a: step 1, ALEX end first, the pads land on the bare ALEX top; b: step 3, back on its legs with the pads in their rings, level, 83.0 cm at both ends. Rail A hangs under the top; it never touches ALEX 1.
!
Push the cabinet, never the top.
Top A is positioned only by moving ALEX 1 under it. Once the rings are on and the pads sit in them, push the ALEX with the pads in the rings; the top moves with it.
2
Fit the rings on ALEX 1 by tracing.
  1. With top A standing where step 1 left it, draw round each of the four pads on the ALEX top with a sharp pencil.
  2. Two people: lift top A off and lay it face down on the blanket.
  3. Check the traces before drilling anything. Lay the ALEX corner template on each bare corner, fences against the edges: the pencil trace must sit inside the template's inner engraved circle (⌀52 mm) all round. If one does not, put the top back on, push the cabinet to correct it, and trace again.
  4. Set a ring (P10) on each pencil circle, flange down. Look down through its open bottom and move it until the pencil line shows all the way round inside the ⌀52 mm bore.
  5. Hold it there. Start the three 5/64 in pilots through the ring's own holes, 3–4 mm deep, lift the ring, and finish each pilot 12 mm deep with the drill guide's 12 ALEX puck.
  6. Put the ring back and drive three H06 #6 × 5/8 in flat-head screws.
Fig. G-2 · P09 foot, P10 ring. a: a ring on its pencil circle, seen from above (1:2); b: the pad drops into the ring, section (1:2.4). Ring bore 52 mm VERIFIED (from its geometry file); pad 50 mm MEASURE ON HARDWARE. The corner-template check (item 3) is made on the bare corner, before any ring is fitted: a flat template cannot lie over a fitted ring.
3
Stand top A again and level it. Two people, ALEX end first as in step 1: the pads drop into the rings. Level along x and along y by turning the OLOV feet and the round feet until it reads flat and all four pads bear with no rock. Target: surface 83.0 cm above the floor at every corner. SOURCE-DEFINED
!
From here on, never push or lift the top alone.
If top A is not 3 cm off a wall (the wall gauge's EKB 30 leg, 3 cm), push the ALEX with the pads in the rings; the top moves with it.

83.0 cm assumes ALEX units 70.0 cm tall ASSUMPTION / FIELD CHECK until measured. With the optional ALEX feet the surface is 85.0, 87.0 or 89.0 cm (2, 4 or 6 cm feet).

4
Top B on the bare ALEX 2, then its rings.
  1. Wind each of top B's OLOV legs to 79.9–80.0 cm (plate face to foot), so rail B's corner end can later slide under top A.
  2. ALEX 2 stands with its near end 282.5 cm from the back wall (chapter F), 5 cm short of its place, so rail B's corner end clears rail A's flat leg. Turn top B over onto its legs with its pads on the bare ALEX 2, ALEX end first. For a moment top B's far end reaches 319 cm from the back wall: the side wall must run at least 322 cm; if it does not, stop and ask.
  3. Push ALEX 2, never the top, until 3.5 cm of top shows all round the cabinet and the wall gauge's ALEX 65 leg (6.5 cm) just fits between the side wall and the ALEX.
  4. Fit the four rings on ALEX 2 by tracing, exactly as in step 2: pencil round each pad, lift top B off, check every trace with the corner template, rings on the circles, pilots through the rings, screws.
  5. Stand top B back on, ALEX end first: the pads drop into the rings.
5
Push ALEX 2 home; top B comes with it. Push ALEX 2 the last 5 cm toward the back wall, with the pads in its rings, until rail B's corner end touches the outer face of rail A's vertical leg and B's end meets A's front edge (y = 65 cm). Under the corner, rail B's outer face lines up with the taped 52.0 cm mark on rail A. Then wind B's legs up to 80.2 cm, until the two surfaces are flush.

!
Never force the corner.
If the corner will not meet or come flush, adjust leg height, not the joint. Never hammer, clamp or lever the tops together. If B's end is off along the joint, push ALEX 2 with the pads in the rings; top B moves with it.
