Build-day checklist

Tick each box in the booklet chapter named in its heading. Tape readings in cm, caliper and drill sizes in mm. Datum: top A's back-left corner (0, 0).

Chapter B · Measure before anything is cut, printed or drilled

  • Both EKBACKEN tops 28 mm thick (calipers). At 27 mm nothing changes; under 27 mm, stop and ask. Never deeper than 21 mm into a top. MEASURE ON HARDWARE
  • Feet pad diameter 50 mm (calipers) before printing the 52 mm bore rings. MEASURE ON HARDWARE
  • ALEX height 70.0 cm (with and without its stock feet). ALEX bottom panel thickness, only if you fit the optional ALEX feet. MEASURE ON HARDWARE
  • Delivered rail lengths (bought as 84 in, 213.4 cm; layout 213 cm) and angle thickness 4.8 mm. MEASURE ON HARDWARE
  • VIVO lower clamp: maximum opening 31 mm or more (28 mm top + 3 mm plate); top arm reach (about 89 mm). MEASURE ON HARDWARE
  • Room: 252 × 317 cm of floor cleared, including the 3 cm wall gaps; back wall 252.0 cm or more, side wall at least 322.0 cm (top B is stood 5 cm out in chapter G); 40 cm free on the open sides; baseboard thickness. If any is short, stop and ask. MEASURE ON HARDWARE
  • Feet load rating (design wants 50 kg or more each). ASSUMPTION / FIELD CHECK

Chapters B and C · Parts and tools on hand

  • Six OLOV legs, 105 mm plate with five 5.5 mm holes on an 85 mm circle; 30 × M4 × 16 mm screws (H01), five per leg.
  • Eight of the twelve 4 in feet: 59.5 mm plate with four 5.2 mm holes 46.8 mm apart; 32 × #8 × 5/8 in pan head (H02).
  • Eight printed rings (52 mm bore, 3 mm wall, 10 mm ring on a 75 × 2.5 mm flange), PETG or ABS, flange-down; 24 × #6 × 5/8 in flat head (H06).
  • Two rails, 2 × 2 × 3/16 in angle, ends deburred; 19 × #10 × 1 in pan head Robertson (H03).
  • Four 10 × 3 in mending plates (two joining, two clamp pads); 8 × #10 × 5/8 in (H04) with #10 washers (H05).
  • Two gusseted 90° strut fittings; 6 × 3/8-16 × 1-1/2 in grade 5 bolts (H08), 12 × 3/8 washers (H09), 6 nylon-insert nuts (H10).
  • Printed tools: edge T-square, leg jig, feet corner jig, ALEX corner template, rail marking gauge, rail drill jig, two fitting plugs, drill guide (tower + 5 pucks), foot height gauge (102 mm), wall gauge (6.5 cm and 3 cm legs).
  • Automatic centre punch, cutting oil; 5/64, 1/8, 7/32 and 7/16 in bits (7/16 cobalt); Robertson #2 bit on a 3 in extension.

Chapter D · Marking both tops, face down

  • Feet F1, F3 (top A) and F5, F6 (top B) at 34.5 cm from the far end, tape hooked on the far end.

Verify before: chapter by chapter

Chapter E · Steel on the bench

  • Rail A stations 3.5, 28.0, 54.0, 80.0, 106.0, 132.0, 158.0, 184.0, 210.5 cm from the wall end (the first at 3.5 cm, not 2.5 cm); rail B 2.5, 7.5, 28.0, 54.0, 80.0, 106.0, 132.0, 158.0, 184.0, 210.5 cm from the corner end; all on the 19 mm line.
  • Fitting as template: rail A heel on the 52.0 cm mark legs toward the far end, and on the 51.5 cm mark legs toward the wall end; rail B heel on the corner end. Bolt holes on the 29 mm line, 7/16 in.
  • Heel marks at 51.5 cm and 52.0 cm scribed on rail A's vertical leg; after painting, the 52.0 cm mark and both rail ends re-taped and re-labelled.
  • Floor test: every bolt slides in by hand.

Chapter F · Legs, feet, rails, cabinets

  • Every OLOV pre-set to 80.2 cm, or the ALEX-feet table's number (decided before this chapter).
  • Every foot set with the 102 mm gauge while it is still loose, before its plate is screwed on: the gauge slides over from the side with no rock.
  • Rail A 12 cm from the front edge to the vertical leg's outer face at both ends and in the middle before drilling; end screws first, re-check, then the rest. Rail pilots 25 mm from the flat leg's top face.
  • Rails screwed to the tops before the tops stand: nine screws in rail A, eight in rail B (holes 3 to 10; holes 1 and 2 wait for chapter H).
  • ALEX units placed by the walls: ALEX 1 back 6.5 cm off the back wall, near side 212.5 cm from the side wall; ALEX 2 side 6.5 cm off the side wall, near end staged at 282.5 cm from the back wall (pushed home to 277.5 cm in chapter G).
  • No rings yet: the rings go on in chapter G.

Chapter G · Standing the tops, rings by tracing

  • Each top stood on its bare ALEX, 3.5 cm of top showing all round (the wall gauge's 6.5 cm leg at the wall); pencil traced round each pad; top lifted off.
  • Each ring set on its circle, pencil line visible all round through the open bottom; pilots 3–4 mm through the ring's own holes, finished with the 12 ALEX puck; three #6 × 5/8 in screws.
  • Top stood back up: all four pads drop into their rings. Top A 3 cm off both walls, level along x and y.
  • Top B's OLOV legs wound to 79.9–80.0 cm before rail B slides under top A, then up to 80.2 cm once home. ALEX 2 pushed home with top B on it: push the ALEX, never the top alone.

Chapter H · The joint

  • Before the plates: rail B's end touches rail A's vertical leg with no gap, and its outer face lines up with the 52.0 cm mark.
  • Surfaces flush by the feet, never by packing under the joining plates.
  • Rail B's screws 1 and 2 driven before the fittings go on.
  • Before the bolts: surfaces flush and rail B touching rail A; inside fitting gusset-down. Six bolts 25 N·m.

Chapter I · Monitor mount

  • Pad plates on their outlines (x 20.0–45.4 cm, y 0.5–8.1 cm; y 20.0–45.4 cm, x 0.5–8.1 cm); clamps finger-tight at x = 40.0 cm and y = 40.0 cm on the Option 1 holes until the crossbar is on.
  • G5 top edge 66.9 cm above the top, under the 67.3 cm limit; if the mount will not reach, lower the G5 (a bottom edge down to 12 cm above the top still clears the ELLOVEN).

Chapter K · Final checks

  • 1 · Surface 83.0 cm ± 0.3 cm at corners 1–6 (85.0 / 87.0 / 89.0 cm with 2 / 4 / 6 cm ALEX feet), then the inside corner (65, 65) last.
  • 2 · Level across the joint reads flat both ways; a fingertip finds no step.
  • 3 · 20 kg (case of water + books) on top B's inside corner, 10 cm in from both edges. Straightedge from L1's line: gap at the corner 0 with no weight, then ≤ 2 mm with the weight; returns, no creak, nothing moved. Never stand or sit on the desk.
  • 4 · All eight pads bear: rocked sideways by hand (never turned), none rocks or lifts.
  • 5 · Straightedge across the joint at the inside corner: step written down: ______ mm.

  • 8 · Knees clear; rail lower edge 75.1 cm from the floor, 12 cm back from the edge.
  • 9 · Both ALEX fronts open fully without touching a foot or a leg.
