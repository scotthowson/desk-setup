G · Standing the tops and fitting the rings

Two people. Both tops lie face down with their legs, feet and rails on (chapter F), every OLOV at 80.2 cm and every pad set with the foot height gauge (102 mm). ALEX 1 stands in its place; ALEX 2 stands 15 cm short of its place, its near end 292.5 cm from the back wall. Neither has rings yet: each ring is placed by tracing round its pad.

1
Stand top A on the bare ALEX 1. With a helper, turn top A over and stand it on its legs with its four pads on the bare ALEX top: lower the ALEX end first, then the leg end. Push the ALEX unit, never the top, until 3.5 cm of top shows all round the cabinet and the wall gauge's ALEX 65 leg (6.5 cm) just fits between the back wall and the ALEX.

!
Push the cabinet, never the top.
Top A is positioned only by moving ALEX 1 under it. Once the rings are on and the pads sit in them, push the ALEX with the pads in the rings; the top moves with it.

2
Fit the rings on ALEX 1 by tracing.
  1. With top A standing where step 1 left it, draw round each of the four pads on the ALEX top with a sharp pencil.
  2. Two people: lift top A off and lay it face down on the blanket.
  3. Set a ring (P10) on each pencil circle, flange down. Look down through its open bottom and move it until the pencil line shows all the way round inside the ⌀52 mm bore.
  4. Hold it there. Start the three 5/64 in pilots through the ring's own holes, 3–4 mm deep, lift the ring, and finish each pilot 12 mm deep with the drill guide's 12 ALEX puck.
  5. Put the ring back and drive three H06 #6 × 5/8 in flat-head screws.
Fig. G-2 · P09 foot, P10 ring. a: a ring on its pencil circle, seen from above (1:2); b: the pad drops into the ring, section (1:2.4). Ring bore 52 mm VERIFIED (from its geometry file); pad 50 mm MEASURE ON HARDWARE. The ALEX corner template only checks the ring positions afterwards.
3
Stand top A again and level it. Two people, ALEX end first as in step 1: the pads drop into the rings. Level along x and along y by turning the OLOV feet and the round feet until it reads flat and all four pads bear with no rock. Target: surface 83.0 cm above the floor at every corner. SOURCE-DEFINED
!
From here on, never push or lift the top alone.
If top A is not 3 cm off a wall (the wall gauge's EKB 30 leg, 3 cm), push the ALEX with the pads in the rings; the top moves with it.

83.0 cm assumes ALEX units 70.0 cm tall ASSUMPTION / FIELD CHECK until measured. With the optional ALEX feet the surface is 85.0, 87.0 or 89.0 cm (2, 4 or 6 cm feet).

4
Top B on the bare ALEX 2, then its rings.
  1. Wind each of top B's OLOV legs to 79.9–80.0 cm (plate face to foot), so rail B's corner end can later slide under top A.
  2. ALEX 2 stands with its near end 292.5 cm from the back wall (chapter F), so rail B's 12 cm projection clears top A. Turn top B over onto its legs with its pads on the bare ALEX 2, ALEX end first.
  3. Push ALEX 2, never the top, until 3.5 cm of top shows all round the cabinet and the wall gauge's ALEX 65 leg (6.5 cm) just fits between the side wall and the ALEX.
  4. Fit the four rings on ALEX 2 by tracing, exactly as in step 2: pencil round each pad, lift top B off, rings on the circles, pilots through the rings, screws.
  5. Stand top B back on, ALEX end first: the pads drop into the rings.
5
Push ALEX 2 home; top B comes with it. Push ALEX 2 toward the back wall, with the pads in its rings, until rail B's corner end touches the outer face of rail A's vertical leg and B's end meets A's front edge (y = 65 cm). Under the corner, rail B's outer face lines up with the taped 52.0 cm mark on rail A. Then wind B's legs up to 80.2 cm, until the two surfaces are flush.

H · The joint

Both tops stand on their rings (chapter G); top B's end meets top A's front edge and rail B's end touches rail A. You work from below, lying in the knee space. The two gusseted fittings bolted round rail B's end, not the joining plates, carry the inside corner of top B.

!
SEQUENCE: THE FITTINGS GO ON LAST SOURCE-DEFINED
1 · The two surfaces are flush at the joint, levelled with the feet, never packed (step 1).
2 · Rail B's end touches rail A's vertical leg; rail B's outer face is on the taped 52.0 cm mark (step 2).
3 · Joining plates P1 and P2 are screwed across the joint (step 3). They align the tops; they do not carry the corner.
4 · Rail B's screws 1 and 2 are in top A (step 4). The inside fitting sits right under them and would block the driver.
5 · Only then the fittings (steps 5–7), and the bolts tightened last (step 8).

Fig. H-5 · H08 bolt, H09 washer, H10 nut, P06/P07 rails, P08 fitting. Bolt stack-ups, cut along the bolt, all mm; plates drawn cut off. The fitting's slots are 21 × 12 mm; the cut shows their 12 mm width. VERIFIED fitting; SOURCE-DEFINED bolts.
8
Check again, then tighten all six. Fingertip across the joint: no step. Rail B's end still touching rail A. Then tighten every bolt fully, as the panel says.
!
TIGHTENING: 25 N·m (18 ft·lb) SOURCE-DEFINED
With a torque wrench, 25 N·m (18 ft·lb), under the standard dry torque even for a grade 2 bolt. Without one, pull as hard as one hand can on a 15 cm wrench, then stop.
Why tight, not snug: this is a friction joint. The fittings' 21 × 12 mm slots leave 2.5 mm of play across and 11.5 mm along each bolt, the 7/16 in rail holes 1.6 mm, so only the clamping force keeps rail B's corner from settling.
When: only after the two surfaces are flush and rail B's end touches rail A.
Later: if the corner ever drops a millimetre, retighten these six bolts.
