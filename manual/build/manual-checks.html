I · Monitor mount and accessories

The VIVO STAND-V300CU (P12) has two independent C-clamps and no base bar. Both clamp onto top A's own wall edges, never on the joint: clamp 1 centred at x = 400 (40.0 cm) on the back edge, clamp 2 centred at y = 400 (40.0 cm) on the side-wall end. The crossbar runs on the diagonal between the poles. Each lower jaw bears on a spare steel mending plate (P11), because particleboard crushes under a bare steel jaw over months. Datum: top A's back-left corner (0, 0); x along the back wall, y along the side wall; mm.

Hang the mount

1

Lay the pad plates. Lay a spare mending plate P11 flat on the underside, on each pad outline drawn when top A was marked, long side along the edge: x 200–454, y 5–81 at clamp 1; y 200–454, x 5–81 at clamp 2. Loose, no screws: the jaw holds it. SOURCE-DEFINED

!
MEASURE ON HARDWARE before hanging the clamps
Top arm reach (about 89) and maximum opening (needs 31 or more: 28 top + 3 plate) are not measured. Open the lower clamp fully on the Option 1 holes and measure the gap; if it is under 31, stop. The clamp zones are drawn 90 deep from the edge; if the reach measures more, keep the zone clear to the measured reach.
2

Hang both lower clamps, finger-tight only. Clamp 1 centred at x = 400 on A's back edge, clamp 2 centred at y = 400 on A's side-wall end (Fig. I-1). Use the VIVO sheet's Option 1 holes (2 and 4), since 28 + 3 = 31 is under 60. The clamp backs sit in the 30 mm wall gap; the lower pad lands on the plate, 13.5–63.5 from the edge (Fig. I-2).

3

Fit the poles and the crossbar as the spacing guide (VIVO sheet step 3). The crossbar measured 502 centre to centre, so it fixes the pole spacing. Only now tighten both clamps, with the crossbar on. VERIFIED

4

Refit the crossbar at monitor height (VIVO sheet step 6): 400–430 above the top (1230–1260 from the floor).

5

Hang the three screens, all landscape, and set the heights in Fig. I-3. The G5 goes on the centre bracket (VESA 75), screen centred on the diagonal at (365, 365), 200 (20 cm) in front of the crossbar. The Acers go on the side arms, toed in 15°; each Acer's near end ends up about 120 (12 cm) from the G5's curved end. The side arms run along the wall behind each panel, then forward into the VESA plate.

J · Final inspection and load testing

Ten checks, in the build manual's order, before you call the desk done. Each is an ACCEPTANCE CHECK: a pass or fail you can see or measure. Several of them test a STRUCTURAL ESTIMATE from the design review that has not been measured on this desk; the check says which. Write the results in the boxes and in the sign-off table at the end of this chapter. Datum for every drawing: top A's back-left corner (0, 0); mm.

Check 1 · Surface height at the six corners ACCEPTANCE CHECK

1

Measure from the floor to the top face at each of the six corners of the L (Fig. J-1). Every reading 830 ± 3 (83.0 cm). With the optional ALEX feet fitted: 830 plus their height. If a corner is out, turn that corner's OLOV foot or round foot, then measure all six again.

Check 3 · Corner load test ACCEPTANCE CHECK

!
NEVER STAND OR SIT ON THE DESK
The test load is a known weight: 20 kg of water and books. Do not stand or sit on the corner; that is not what the beam was sized for.
Fig. J-3a Plan. Case faces 100 in from top B's front edge and from the joint (case size schematic). R: reading point.
3

Set 20 kg (a case of water plus a few books) on top B's inside corner, 100 (10 cm) in from both edges.

Rest a straightedge from leg L1's line across the corner. Read the drop at the corner (R): no more than 2. The estimate is about 1. STRUCTURAL ESTIMATE

Lift the weight off. The corner returns to where it was, nothing creaks, no screw or bolt has moved.

Tests: sag at the inside corner (rail A carrying rail B's corner reaction) and corner-joint slip (the six fitting bolts holding by friction). STRUCTURAL ESTIMATE

Check 4 · All eight pads bear ACCEPTANCE CHECK

4

At each of the eight feet F1–F8, try to turn the pad in its ring by hand. None spins free or rocks. A pad that spins or rocks is not bearing: see chapter K, "Top moves on its ALEX".

Tests: the racking estimate (eight pads captive in their rings hold the frame against a sideways push) and the ALEX load share. STRUCTURAL ESTIMATE

Check 6 · Sideways push ACCEPTANCE CHECK

6

Push the desk sideways along each wall. No sway at the top beyond a few millimetres, and no top moves on its ALEX.

Tests: racking. The OLOV legs give almost no racking resistance; the two ALEX units, locked to the tops by the pads in their rings, do. STRUCTURAL ESTIMATE

Fig. J-6 Push along the back wall, then along the side wall.

Check 7 · Screw tips ACCEPTANCE CHECK

7

No screw tip visible, or felt as a bump, on either laminate face. Run a hand over both tops, above every leg, foot plate, rail screw and joining plate.

Check 8 · Knees clear ACCEPTANCE CHECK

8

Knees clear. Sit at the corner and slide in: nothing within reach of a knee or thigh. The rails' lower edge is 751 (75.1 cm) from the floor, 120 (12 cm) back from the front edge.

!
NEVER DEEPER THAN 21 MM INTO A TOP
The tops are 28 mm particleboard with a thin laminate skin; a screw reaching the skin lifts it. Replace a screw only with the same size. If a top measured 27, every screw into it is 1 mm shorter. SOURCE-DEFINED
