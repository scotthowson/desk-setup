# Build manual

Every number you need, in build order. Coordinates are (x, y) in cm measured from the back-left corner of top A: x runs along the back wall, y along the side wall. Work on the undersides with the tops face down on a blanket; a helper is needed for steps 6 and 7. Budget a weekend: steel prep on day one, assembly on day two.

The interactive 3D model is at `index.html` (open it in a browser, or enable GitHub Pages on this repo and open the site). The full design review with the structural reasoning is in `design-review.md`.

## 0. Before you start

- [ ] Both EKBACKEN tops measure 28 mm thick (calipers). If 27 mm, shorten every screw into the top by 1 mm.
- [ ] Seven OLOV legs on hand (six ordered plus one more).
- [ ] Eight round 4 in feet (four per ALEX). If you only have four, buy four more before step 4.
- [ ] Two 7 ft lengths of 2 × 2 × 3/16 in angle plus a 1 ft offcut, deburred.
- [ ] Four 10 × 3 in mending plates, 19 × #10 × 1 in screws, 16 × #10 × ⅝ in screws with washers, 4 × ¼-20 × ¾ in bolts, nuts, 8 washers.
- [ ] VIVO pole spacing measured centre to centre on the crossbar (the plan assumes 42 cm).
- [ ] Room cleared: the desk needs 254 × 319 cm of floor including the 5 cm wall gaps.

## 4. Fit legs and feet to both tops

1. Pilot every leg plate hole 1/8 in, 15 mm deep, then drive the IKEA screws. Pre-set every OLOV to 80.2 cm (measure plate face to foot).
2. Pilot the feet plate holes 1/8 in, 15 mm deep; screw each plate to the top, pad pointing away from the top. Wind each pad out until the foot measures 10.2 cm overall.
3. Rail A: clamp it on its line, wall end flush with x = 0. Through each rail hole, drill a 1/8 in pilot 20 mm into the top (tape on the bit at 25 mm). Drive #10 × 1 in screws until the head seats. Nine screws.
4. Rail B: clamp it on its line with the corner end projecting 11 cm past the top's joint end. Pilot and screw holes 3 to 10 only (eight screws). Holes 1 and 2 wait for step 7.

## 5. Place the ALEX units

| Unit | Footprint (x, y) | Faces |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ALEX 1 drawers | x 209.5–245.5, y 3.5–61.5 | drawers toward you (+y) |
| ALEX 2 storage | x 3.5–61.5, y 274.5–310.5 | door toward you (+x) |

Stand them 5 cm off the walls like the tops. A strip of painter's tape on the floor at x = 209.5 and y = 274.5 saves guessing.

## 6. Stand top A (two people)

Lift top A onto L1–L4 with the four feet pads landing on ALEX 1. Push it to 5 cm off both walls. Put a level on the top along x and along y; adjust the OLOV feet and the round feet until it reads flat and all four pads bear with no rock. Target: top surface 83.0 cm above the floor at every corner.

## 7. Stand top B and make the joint

1. Lift top B onto L5–L7 and ALEX 2, slide its end against A's front edge, 5 cm off the side wall.
2. Adjust its legs and feet until the two surfaces are flush at the joint (run a fingertip across; shim the legs, never the plates).
3. From below, screw plates P1 and P2 across the joint: #10 × ⅝ in with washers, 1/8 in pilots 12 mm deep, 4 screws per plate, two in each top.
4. Push rail B's corner end against rail A's vertical leg. Drill its holes 1 and 2 into top A (pilots 20 mm) and screw.
5. Clamp the 9 cm bracket into the outside corner of the two vertical legs, heel at the corner. Drill 9/32 in through the bracket using the rail holes as guides, four holes. Bolt with a washer each side and nylon-insert nuts, snug.

## 9. Checks before you call it done

- [ ] Top surface 83.0 cm at all six outer corners (± 3 mm).
- [ ] A level across the joint reads flat both ways.
- [ ] Push the inside corner down hard: it should give 2–3 mm and spring back, no creak.
- [ ] Push the desk sideways along each wall: no sway at the top beyond a few millimetres.
- [ ] No screw tip visible or felt as a bump on either laminate face.
- [ ] Knees clear: sit at the corner, slide in, nothing within reach of a knee or thigh.
- [ ] Both ALEX fronts open fully without touching a foot or a leg.
