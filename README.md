# desk-setup

L-shaped PC desk built from two IKEA EKBACKEN countertops (249 × 65 × 2.8 cm) on two ALEX units, seven OLOV legs and two 2 × 2 × 3/16 in steel angle rails, with a VIVO STAND-V300CU triple monitor mount across the inside corner (Samsung Smart M7 32 in centre, two Acer XZ320QU 31.5 in curved sides).

## Key numbers

- Top surface 83.0 cm (ALEX 70 + 4 in feet 10.16 + top 2.8); every OLOV set to 80.2 cm.
- ALEX units centred under the wing ends with 3.5 cm of top showing all round.
- Rails 12 cm back from the front edges, bolted together at the inside corner; under-rail clearance 75.1 cm.
- VIVO clamps at (35, 0) and (0, 35) on top A's edges, poles 42 cm apart on the diagonal (to confirm against the mount).
- Tops stand 5 cm off both walls so the clamp backs fit.

Coordinates throughout are (x, y) in cm from the back-left corner of top A: x along the back wall, y along the side wall.
